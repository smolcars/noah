Tap the balance for details; long-press it (or use Hide balance) to hide it. Tap a nudge's button to see it resolve and the next one slide in. Scroll Everyday to see the balance tuck into the top bar.

The three moments

  • Fresh: ₿0 and your logo QR front and centre, ready to share. No empty “No activity yet” list; the empty state is the way to get paid.
  • First payment: the QR card turns into an orange ring and check, the amount and sender appear, the balance rolls up digit by digit, success haptic. Then the card folds away and Recent takes its place, with the new row marked by a small orange dot.
  • Everyday: balance, fiat, then Recent (five rows, See all goes to Activity) and the small map row. The QR lives in the Receive tab from then on.
  • Busy: an arriving deposit under the balance with a slow pulse, pending and failed rows, and the renew nudge.
  • Rows: people and addresses get an initial; deposits, Lightning merchants and recurring payments get an icon. Pending rows get a slow dashed ring, failed ones a small red badge, and a new incoming payment a small orange dot.

Nudges

  • One at a time, in this order: renew bitcoin, back up your wallet, add your email, update available.
  • Renew shows only inside the 24-hour maintenance window, when automatic renewal hasn't happened yet. No earlier warning.
  • Backup clears only when you confirm after seeing the phrase. Email can be dismissed with ×.
  • Resolving one folds it away and slides the next one in.

Top bar

  • Left: you. An orange-ringed initial and your name; tap for your codes (today's QR hub). Signet and regtest builds show a small network badge here.
  • Right: scan and settings. The map moves out of the toolbar into the smaller map row.
  • Scrolling tucks the balance into the centre of the top bar.

Wide and foldable

  • iPhone Duo open, Pixel Fold open, tablets: two panes split at the fold. Left: you, balance, nudge, map. Right: the get-paid card when fresh, otherwise the activity list grouped by day.
  • The tab bar sits in the left pane, so nothing crosses the fold when the device is bent. Sheets open in the right pane.
  • Closed Duo and Pixel Fold closed use the phone layout.

Platform

  • iOS 26+: Liquid Glass on the top-bar controls, the nudge, the map row, Copy, the sheet and the native floating tab bar. Older iOS falls back to materials.
  • Android: the same layout with flat tonal surfaces and the Material 3 navigation bar, restyled monochrome.

Motion

  • Digits roll with snappy and a light blur; hide morphs digits into dots with bouncy.
  • Check ring and the card folding away use soft; rows slide in with snappy, 40 ms apart.
  • Reduce Motion turns all of it into 180 ms fades.
