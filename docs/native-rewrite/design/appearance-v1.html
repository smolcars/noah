Where it lives

A new Appearance row in Settings → Display, after Language. Its value reads Automatic, Light or Dark. It defaults to Automatic, so nothing changes for people who upgrade.

The sheet

  • Three tiny Home screens: your real balance, your initial in the orange ring, Recent rows and the tab bar. If amounts are hidden, the balance shows dots.
  • Automatic is cut diagonally, light on one side and dark on the other. The line under it says what your phone is on right now.
  • The selected tile gets the same 2pt outline and check as the Bitcoin unit sheet. Liquid Glass on iOS, Material on Android.

The moment

  • Tap a tile and the whole app re-colours in a circle that grows out of the tile, with a selection haptic. Try it on the left.
  • The sheet stays open so you can compare. The other pickers close on their own after a tap.
  • With Reduce Motion on, it's a plain cross-fade.

Scope

  • Covers every screen, sheet, alert and the keyboard.
  • Widgets, notifications and the launch screen draw outside the app, so they keep following the phone.
  • On the Duo and Fold the sheet sits in the trailing pane, and the tiles grow to fill it.
