“Don’t Allow” leads to the required state: the only way forward is turning notifications on. Taken names: satoshi, noah, bitcoin, hal. Restore checks words and the BIP-39 checksum for real; the sample phrase is the public test vector.

Where the tree appears

  • Only in onboarding: the welcome screen, the creating and restoring moments, and the notifications screen, where it explains why they are required. Nowhere in the everyday app.
  • One later candidate, if you want it: emergency exit, where your branch drops to the Bitcoin line.
  • The drawing follows bark: radix-4 branches, leaves are VTXOs, your exit branch in orange, trees expire after about 30 days.

Email

  • Optional, like today: Skip stays. Used only for urgent wallet alerts.
  • Domain completion and typo fixes (“gmial.com”). Type 000000 to see a wrong code.
  • iOS fills the code from Mail above the number pad; the six boxes fold into a check.

QR

  • Encodes a real BIP-321 URI (sample addresses) at error correction H; the logo covers about 5% of it, well within what H can lose.
  • Always on a white card, so it scans in dark mode too.
  • Native: CoreImage on iOS and ZXing on Android (already a dependency) produce the matrix; we draw the rounded modules, eyes and logo ourselves.
