Ark-native motion

  • Your coin. Ark money is coins (VTXOs). Yours have an orange ring; the recipient's piece is solid. No trees here: those stay in onboarding.
  • Ark send: split, sign, deliver. Your coin splits into the payment and your change (bark's checkpoint fans one coin out). You (person) and the Ark server each draw a signature onto the payment. It drops into their mailbox. Instant, and Ark sends are free.
  • Lightning send: lock, pay, unlock. The payment is padlocked so only a secret can release it, and tied to the Ark server, which holds it. Ark pays over Lightning (the bolt). Paying hands the payee a receipt: their secret, the preimage. It travels back through Ark as a key, the lock opens, and Ark takes the piece it paid for. If it fails, no key ever comes: Ark signs the piece back to you as a fresh coin.
  • On-chain send: swap, then blocks. Ark pays the address from its own coins in exchange for yours. Your transaction (orange) waits in a blinking “next” block after the latest mined blocks, mempool.space style, with real heights from the explorer. When it's mined: “Confirmed in block 921,346”. Block times are sped up here.
  • Hold to send. Your finger signs: an orange stroke traces the button while you hold, with haptic ticks. Let go early and it rewinds.

Receive

  • Listening. A thin orange light circles the card while it can be paid. Lightning needs Noah awake to claim, so this is literal.
  • Your Lightning address is the default QR. Add amount switches it to the unified payment request (Bitcoin and Ark addresses, the amount and a Lightning invoice in one code) with an amount band. Every format is in the list (Copy on phones, the right pane on the Duo): Lightning address, payment request, Lightning invoice, Ark address, Bitcoin address. Tap one to show it.
  • Ark arrives from the Ark server (your mailbox) as a coin dropped into the card. Lightning hits the server, which hands you the payment locked to your secret; your phone's key opens it. On-chain waits in the next block under the card, then shows the block it confirmed in.
  • Then the card flips to the amount with the success haptic, and flips back.
  • Add amount grows an inverted band on the card and the QR redraws with a Lightning invoice inside.

Wide and foldable

  • Send: the amount and the motion stage on the left, the keypad, recipients and the hold button on the right.
  • Receive: the card unfolds along the hinge: QR on the left, every format with Copy on the right. No sheets.
