Tap a row to open it; Back returns it to its place. Drag the list down at the top to refresh. The ⋯ menu holds the filter, Export CSV and Refresh.

List

  • The same row as Home's Recent: initials for people and Lightning addresses, icons for Lightning without a name, on-chain, recurring, renewals and plain Ark payments. Monochrome amounts.
  • Pending rows sit together at the top under “Pending”, with Home's slow dashed ring. Then days: Today, Yesterday, dates. Day headers stick under the bar.
  • Failed rows get the red badge; a refunded Lightning send says “back in your wallet”. Canceled rows are struck through, without the badge.
  • New incoming rows keep Home's orange dot until they have been on screen for a moment, then it shrinks away. Memory only, shared with Home.
  • Search matches names, notes, amounts and IDs. The filter (All, Lightning, Ark, On-chain) lives in ⋯ and shows as a chip next to the title while it's on.

Detail

  • The row grows into the screen: its avatar, amount and name fly to their places in the header.
  • One status line, the value at the time, then To or From, Via, Fee, Total, Date, Note. Proof of payment for Lightning sends, the transaction ID with the explorer for on-chain.
  • Pending on-chain payments show Send's block row: the orange dot waits in the next block, then the confirmations count up.
  • Details holds everything else today's screen shows: payment hash, invoice, IDs, movement data, coins (each opens coin details), raw transaction and metadata.
  • Pay again when today's rules allow it.

Export

  • ⋯ → Export CSV opens a short confirmation that names how many payments the file holds and what's in it, then the system share sheet. Same columns as today.

Wide and foldable

  • iPhone Duo open: the list and tab bar in the leading pane, the detail in the trailing pane. Nothing crosses the fold; switching rows rolls the amount.
  • Android fold open: the navigation rail, the list, then the detail on the other side of the hinge. Sheets open in the trailing pane.

Motion

  • Row to detail and back: snappy. Sheets and the menu: snappy. Details opening: soft. The new dot: soft. The amount on wide: Home's digit roll.
  • Reduce Motion turns all of it into 180 ms fades and stops the ring, pulse and blink.
