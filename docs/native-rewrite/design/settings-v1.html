Start on Home and tap the gear: Settings zooms out of it and your initial moves into the header. Everything is tappable; Back or Esc returns. Codes that start with 0 fail; the name “satoshi” is taken. The recovery phrase is a fake sample.

Settings

  • Not a tab: the gear on Home opens it, zooming out of the button. The name chip still opens My code; both show the same “you”: the orange-ringed initial.
  • One list, grouped: you, Security, Bitcoin, Display, App, Help, then Delete wallet on its own. Rows are a title and a value. Explanations live one level down, in one line.
  • Orange marks what needs you (an unsaved phrase, coins to renew). Red is only for failures and Delete.

Safety gates

  • Recovery phrase: Face ID or the device passcode every time (Android: biometric or screen lock), and the “No screen lock” warning when the phone has none. The words are never in the screen until then, and clear when you leave.
  • Delete wallet: type “delete”. The sheet shows what you'd need to restore before you do.
  • Emergency exit, claims and renewals: the fees and warnings first, then Send's hold to confirm.

Wide and foldable

  • iPhone Duo open: the list and the glass tab bar in the leading pane, the chosen page in the trailing pane. Sheets open there too. Nothing crosses the fold.
  • Android fold open: the navigation rail, the list, then the page on the other side of the hinge.

Motion

  • Push and back: snappy, with the platform's own slide (iOS) or shared axis (Android). Switches, chips and sheets: snappy. The phrase reveal, backup check and Details: soft. Checks and counters: bouncy.
  • Reduce Motion turns all of it into 180 ms fades and stops the rings and pulses.
